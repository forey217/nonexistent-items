正式接入本原型的文件：

`bureau_props_v01.png`为未使用的早期图集，已从导出资源中排除。

## 人物提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Produce a GAME-READY TRANSPARENT SPRITE SHEET for the heroine Yanyue, based on the approved art board supplied as reference. Preserve the heroine from the TOP LEFT of that board: adult East Asian woman, black fringe and twin buns, blue crystal bun ornaments and dangling blue earrings, white long-sleeve turtleneck, black belted pleated skirt, dark translucent tights, chunky black ankle boots with blue crystal details. Same clean 2D anime ink lines and restrained cel shading, approximately five-to-six-head full-body proportions. Natural nonsexual walking, relaxed arms. Do not copy the board's background, text, Tesla or furniture.
OUTPUT: a square sprite atlas, ideally 2048x2048, with EXACTLY FOUR ROWS and FOUR COLUMNS, 16 evenly spaced uniform cells. Fully transparent RGBA background; no white backdrop, no drawn grid, no labels, no cast shadow, no floor, no decorative content.
Camera is the same elevated isometric / three-quarter top-down gameplay camera for every cell, showing the top of the head and shoulders. Keep identical camera, scale, outfit, face and anatomy throughout. Each complete figure is centered within its cell, with generous padding and both feet near the same baseline. Never clip head, boots or hair.
ROW 1: facing lower-right / southeast, front three-quarter face visible.
ROW 2: facing lower-left / southwest, front three-quarter face visible.
ROW 3: facing upper-right / northeast, back three-quarter, back of head and outfit visible.
ROW 4: facing upper-left / northwest, back three-quarter, back of head and outfit visible.
For EACH row, COLUMN 1 is a neutral balanced stationary pose with feet together; COLUMN 2 is left foot forward/right foot back with subtle opposed arm swing; COLUMN 3 is a centered passing stride; COLUMN 4 is right foot forward/left foot back with opposite arm swing. Movement remains small and believable, retaining consistent height and identity. All figures in a row keep exactly that row's facing. This sheet will be sliced into a 4x4 grid in Godot. Absolute priority is clean transparency, consistent cell registration, four unambiguous facings and complete figures.
```

## 家具提示词

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create one TRANSPARENT 2x2 prop atlas for a 2D isometric game, visually matching the approved reference board's BOTTOM RIGHT observation bureau room. Same clean detailed 2D anime illustration / comic cel shading, warm dark wood, charcoal metal, restrained antique gold trim and blue observation crystal accents. NOT 3D, not photographic, no characters.
Square canvas ideally 1536x1536 or 2048x2048, EXACTLY TWO ROWS and TWO COLUMNS, four equally sized cells, generous transparent margins. Each prop isolated on truly transparent RGBA, no room background, no lettering, no grid and no external cast shadow. All four objects share an elevated isometric camera with vertical lines upright and floor axes descending left/right at about 27 degrees. Objects centered in each cell and complete, base resting at bottom center.
TOP LEFT: a small dark wooden bureau desk with modest gold trim, a closed ledger and a small warm desk lamp. No chair, no rug, no person.
TOP RIGHT: a freestanding dark wooden bookcase containing a few blue and cream books, closed side panels, no loose floating items.
BOTTOM LEFT: a compact sealed wooden storage chest with dark metal edges, closed lid, no text.
BOTTOM RIGHT: a compact observation pedestal with a contained luminous blue orb inside two simple brass rings, substantial dark base. This is generic test-room scenery, not a canonical story device.
Prop silhouettes should be crisp and readable at small game scale; retain the approved board's visual language. No extra sub-panels or repeated props.
```

Output square 1280x1280 or another square size, exact 2 columns x 2 rows. Top left desk; top right bookcase; bottom left chest; bottom right observation globe. Each prop must occupy at most 72 percent of its quadrant's width AND 72 percent of its quadrant's height, centered in its quadrant. No part of an object may approach or cross the central vertical or horizontal dividing line. At least 60 transparent pixels separate every object from every cell edge, with particularly large empty bands around the central horizontal line. Keep ALL bases and legs complete. Fully transparent RGBA, no background, no shadow, no text, no grid. Do not redesign objects or introduce characters.
